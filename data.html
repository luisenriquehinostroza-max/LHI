What data you'd need to provide

This page shows, module by module, what information was used to build the interactive demo (with sample data), and what kind of information a real producer would need to provide for the platform to work with their own operation. Most of this data likely already exists in a producer's current records (spreadsheets, field notebooks, or accounting system) — even if it's not in Odoo yet.

The short version

In essence, the platform needs four kinds of information most producers already track in some form: (1) start dates for each batch/stage, (2) harvest and production volumes, (3) sales history, and (4) per-unit costs. Nothing new to collect — Vinya simply organizes and compares what you already have to generate the alerts automatically.

Vinya is a project in development by Hinos Investments LLC, Orlando, Florida.
