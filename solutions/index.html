Know who's about to cancel before they do.

Wine clubs lose 25 to 45 percent of their members every year, and nearly 40 percent cancel in the first twelve months. Most small wineries never see it coming, because the warning signs sit buried in order history nobody has time to read.

Vinya watches your club members' ordering patterns and flags the ones going quiet: skipped shipments, lapsed payment cards, declining order frequency. Every week you get a simple list of at-risk members, each with a ready-to-send win-back email drafted for you. You review it. You hit send. Nothing goes out on autopilot.

  • Built for the store you already have. Works with Bloom and Shopify. No migration, no 4 to 6 week launch project.
  • A saved member is worth about $630 a year. Saving even a handful of would-be cancellations pays for the year.
  • You stay in control. Every win-back email is drafted, never sent, until you approve it.
